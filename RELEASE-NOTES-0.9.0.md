PC Tweaker Uninstaller 0.9.0 makes your software inventory easier to explore, with explicit sorting and focused filters.

- Sort by program name, reported size, reported date, publisher or source, in either direction.
- Natural name ordering keeps Tool 2 before Tool 10. Unknown sizes, dates and publishers stay at the bottom.
- Combine search with source, removal guidance, Large and Recent filters. Invalid and future dates no longer enter Recent.
- Keep your sort and compact-row preference between launches; reset the view in one click.
- Read reported-size totals for the current view and see how many apps have no reported size. Dates can reflect servicing; size estimates are not a promise of recoverable space.
- New controls are available in English, Italian, French, Spanish and German.

Windows application and installer publisher signatures are verified before distribution, alongside the separate updater signatures. Existing uninstall confirmations and product-specific entitlements are preserved.
