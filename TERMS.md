PC Tweaker Uninstaller is a Windows desktop app that lists installed programs, runs their uninstallers, and helps you find and remove leftovers they leave behind — with a safety net: reversible changes are snapshotted before they are applied, and leftover files are moved to a quarantine backed by the Recycle Bin rather than permanently deleted.
