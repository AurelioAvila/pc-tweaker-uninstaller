PC Tweaker Uninstaller 0.14.0 adds a Support panel and subscription management in the account menu, and opens at the same size as PC Tweaker.

- The window now opens at the same size as PC Tweaker and remembers where you left it. Every screen also fits a smaller 820 x 600 window.
- Plans & pricing shows the plans straight away, with any current offer on one slim line above them.
- A new Support entry next to Plans & pricing opens Help & support: contact support, copy diagnostic info for a support request (no email address or program names), or open the log folder.
- Manage subscription in the account menu shows your plan, price, renewal date and card. You can cancel at the end of the paid period, with an optional reason, or resume your subscription.
- Subscribers now see the price they actually pay on the Pro card.
- Buy me a coffee now sits at the bottom of the account menu.
- Interface polish: buttons, menus and labels no longer get selected by accident, colours follow your theme everywhere, and text on accent buttons has stronger contrast.
