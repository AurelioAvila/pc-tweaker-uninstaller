PC Tweaker Uninstaller 0.15.0 finds the leftovers of programs you removed earlier, cleans leftover files for free, and lets you undo any cleanup.

- Programs removed earlier: a new Removed programs list remembers programs that disappeared from your installed list, however they were removed, and offers to scan any of them for leftovers. You can also scan by a program's name or its old folder.
- Cleaning leftover files and folders is now free, and they go to the Recycle Bin. Cleaning registry keys and startup entries still needs Pro.
- Leftovers of all users: a program's folders in Program Files and ProgramData and its registry keys under HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE can now be cleaned, with a single Windows permission prompt. If anything changes while Windows asks, nothing is changed.
- Undo is always free: every leftover cleanup can be undone from the Removal Ledger. Registry keys come back exactly as they were, and anything created again in the meantime is never overwritten.
- Only items that come from the removed program's own install folder are pre-selected. Items matched by name only are shown, marked as such, and never selected for you.
- Pro: a PC whose clock runs a day ahead now keeps Pro until the very end of the plan.
- German now uses the informal "du" throughout, and the scrollbars follow your theme everywhere.
